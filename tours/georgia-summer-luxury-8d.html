Day by Day

Day by Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival in Tbilisi

Meet at Tbilisi Airport and transfer to your hotel. Evening orientation / city introduction.

Day 2 — Caucasus mountains

Full-day Caucasus mountain excursion; return to Tbilisi for the night.

Day 3 — Kutaisi & town tour

Travel to Kutaisi (Stepantsminda) with a scenic route and local sightseeing.

Day 4 — To Batumi

Transfer to Batumi on the Black Sea coast.

Day 5 — Batumi Boulevard

Explore Batumi Boulevard and the seaside promenade.

Day 6 — Makhuntseti Waterfall

Day trip to Makhuntseti Waterfall and surroundings.

Day 7 — Martvili & return to Tbilisi

Visit Martvili Canyon / waterfalls area, then transfer back to Tbilisi.

Day 8 — Departure

Airport transfer and farewell.
