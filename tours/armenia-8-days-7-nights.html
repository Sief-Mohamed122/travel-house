Day by Day

Day by Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival in Yerevan

Arrive at Yerevan airport. Pick up luggage and local SIM cards, transfer to hotel, check in. Short rest, light orientation walk.

Day 2 — Yerevan City Tour

Mother Armenia statue panoramic view; English Garden; Cascade arts complex; local markets; Dalma Garden Mall.

Day 3 — Tsaghkadzor & Lake Sevan

"Valley of Flowers"; cable car; optional zipline; Lake Sevan & Sevanavank Monastery; local market; optional fish dinner.

Day 4 — Dilijan – Armenia's Green Heart

Haghartsin Monastery; Deer Reserve; Parz Lake; lunch local restaurant; return to Yerevan.

Day 5 — Garni Village & Geghard

Garni Temple; "Symphony of Stones"; UNESCO Geghard Monastery; traditional Armenian bread baking; free evening.

Day 6 — Khor Virap & Areni Cave

Khor Virap Monastery with Mount Ararat view; Areni Cave; southern Armenia nature.

Day 7 — Shopping & Free Day

Free shopping tour; open time in the city.

Day 8 — Departure

Transfer to Yerevan airport. Safe travels!
